Where it broke

Every pipeline breaks. These are the places mine did, and what I changed.

Auto-replies scored as interest

Out-of-office replies counted as real responses. Now they're filtered before anything is logged.

Bounces failed silently

Dead addresses sat in the pipeline looking healthy until I added a bounce check to the audits.

Enrichment overwrote live contacts

Now it fires at one stage only and never touches an active thread.

Half the pipeline was a wall

Over half my pipeline sat on a store platform we couldn't onboard. None of it converted, and that changed how I qualify.
